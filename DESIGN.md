# Intacto Welty Design

## Surface

Responsive Spanish-language homepage for residential and business recycling inquiries in Misiones.

## Visual Direction

An institutional circular-economy system: clean white and soft-gray fields, anchored by Intacto green (`#1b5e3f`) and lighter action green (`#4a9d6f`). Labeled placeholder frames reserve image locations until approved brand photography is available. IBM Plex Sans keeps service information practical and readable.

## Layout

- A sticky header includes a contrasting top contact strip and leads to named section anchors.
- The hero presents the rotating transformation outcome with a clear recycling CTA, without background imagery.
- Editorial two-column sections alternate with quiet gray service and news bands.
- Cards are reserved for the two services, press items, and certification states.
- Mobile collapses grids to one column and exposes an accessible navigation toggle.

## Components

- Primary, secondary, outline, and text action links use the same 6px control radius.
- Lucide line icons provide a consistent visual language.
- The certification rail advances every five seconds and also has keyboard-accessible arrow controls.
- The contact form validates required browser fields and confirms local submission state.

## Accessibility

Semantic landmarks, heading order, alt text, visible control focus, native labels, responsive type, and high-contrast green actions are present. Press and social URLs remain placeholders until verified destinations are provided.
