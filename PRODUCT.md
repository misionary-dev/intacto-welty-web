# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite and Tailwind CSS, specified by the homepage brief.

## Users

Residents of Misiones looking for a place to recycle used oil, and businesses that need responsible collection and management of used oils and non-ferrous metals.

## Product Purpose

Intacto Welty leads circular-economy recovery in Misiones, turning used oils and non-ferrous metals into valuable products, green employment, and environmental benefit.

## Positioning

The company combines collection, recovery, transformation, and sustainability certification for residential and business recycling in Misiones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The first deliverable is a static, responsive homepage prototype. It must make the recycling, business inquiry, ecological collection-point, and contact paths clear. External press links are placeholders until verified URLs are supplied.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Intacto Welty, clear Spanish language, IBM Plex Sans, and the provided institutional green palette. The homepage uses simple, accessible, semantic interaction patterns.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied homepage brief contains confirmed service descriptions, contact details, certifications, and press headlines. No logo files, verified press URLs, production photography, or map embed are available yet.

## Product Principles

- Make circular-economy services understandable at a glance.
- Give residential and business visitors direct routes to act.
- Present environmental responsibility with clear, grounded language.
- Keep the prototype modular so content can be revised section by section.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, descriptive image alt text, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, responsive layouts, and WCAG AA color contrast.
